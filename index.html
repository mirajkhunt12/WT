Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To become a leading center of excellence in education, research and innovation by developing technically skilled, ethical and socially responsible professionals.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through innovative teaching, practical learning, research and industry interaction while encouraging entrepreneurship and professional values.

Program Specific Outcomes

PSO 1

Technical Knowledge

Apply knowledge of programming, software development, databases and computer technologies to solve engineering problems.

PSO 2

Problem Solving

Analyze real-world problems and develop efficient, innovative and technology-based solutions.

PSO 3

Professional Skills

Demonstrate communication, teamwork, leadership and professional skills required by the software industry.
